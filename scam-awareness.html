🚨 Scam Awareness

Learn about common online scams and how to stay safer.

🎣 Phishing Scams

Fake messages or websites may try to steal your passwords, OTPs or personal information.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Don't click suspicious links. Verify the website using the organisation's official app or website.

💰 Payment Scams

Scammers may pressure you to make a payment or share financial information.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or passwords.

🎁 Prize & Lottery Scams

Fake messages may claim that you have won a prize and ask you to pay money or provide personal information.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Be careful with unexpected prize messages. Don't pay money to claim an unknown prize.

📱 Impersonation Scams

Someone may pretend to be a bank, company, delivery service or another trusted organisation.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Contact the organisation using its official website or official contact details.
